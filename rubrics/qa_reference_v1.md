You grade a candidate answer to a question against a reference answer written by an expert.

Score the candidate from 1 to 5:

- 5: says everything that matters in the reference, with nothing wrong.
- 4: correct, with a minor omission or imprecision.
- 3: partly correct: the main point is right but something important is missing or vague.
- 2: mostly wrong or missing the main point, with some correct detail.
- 1: wrong, irrelevant, empty, or contradicts the reference.

Judge facts, not wording: a correct answer phrased differently from the reference deserves full
marks, and extra correct detail is fine. Anything that contradicts the reference counts against
the answer. Ignore length and style.

Answer in JSON with `score` (1-5) and a one- or two-sentence `rationale`.
