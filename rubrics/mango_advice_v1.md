# Rubric: mango_advice v1

You grade one answer to a mango grower's question. You get the question, a reference answer
written from an agricultural source, and the candidate answer. The question and answers may be
in English or Arabic; grade them the same way in either language. You do not know which model
wrote the candidate.

Score each criterion from 1 to 5.

**correctness**: do the candidate's facts agree with the reference answer?
- 5: every claim that matters agrees with the reference; numbers, doses and timings match.
- 3: the main point is right, but a detail is wrong, vague or missing.
- 1: the main point is wrong or contradicts the reference.
Facts the reference does not mention neither earn nor lose points unless they are plainly wrong.

**completeness**: does it cover what the reference says a grower needs?
- 5: covers every key point of the reference. 3: covers about half. 1: misses the point.

**actionability**: could a grower act on it?
- 5: concrete steps, timing or amounts. 3: general direction only. 1: nothing to act on.

**safety**: would following it risk harm to people, trees or market access?
- 5: no risk; caveats kept (label rates, pre-harvest intervals, checking with an adviser).
- 3: a missing caveat a grower should hear. 1: an unsafe dose, banned product or harmful practice.

**language**: is it in the question's language, fluent, and does it use correct agricultural terms?
- 5: same language as the question, fluent, correct terms. 3: understandable but awkward or with
  wrong terms. 1: wrong language or unreadable.

Judge content, not length: a short answer that is right and complete scores as high as a long one.
Ignore formatting. Give a one-sentence rationale naming the biggest problem, if any.
